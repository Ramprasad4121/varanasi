# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting criticism — technical, not personal
* Focusing on what is best for the community and the protocol's users
* Being kind to newcomers: "RTFM" is never an answer, a link is

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* Sexualized language or advances; trolling, insulting/derogatory comments
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information (physical or electronic address)
  without explicit permission — doxxing ends participation here
* Dismissing security reports as "just a hackathon project"
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable
standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits,
code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to
this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub issues,
discussions, PRs, the project Discord/Telegram if any), and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainer via the contact route in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) (subject line `varanasi-coc`). All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Privacy and security
of the reporter will be respected.

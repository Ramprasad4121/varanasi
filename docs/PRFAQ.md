## Press release (draft, not issued)

**Hyderabad, [launch date TBD].** Varanasi today opened its waitlist for a phone app that lets families in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh save in pure gold kept at a bank, and join chit groups where every rupee and every auction is visible to every member.

Two of the most common ways Indian families save are gold and chits. Both run on trust, and both can break it. Gold bought in small amounts is hard to verify and costly to store safely. Chits are run by a foreman who holds the money, and members rarely see who bid what or where the pot went. When a foreman disappears, families lose years of savings.

Varanasi is built so that you do not have to trust us, or anyone, blindly. With Varanasi gold, each gram you see in the app is backed by gold held at a partner bank branch, with a printed certificate number you can check, and the app shows in plain words how much gold the bank holds against how much the app has issued. With Varanasi chits, every member sees every payment, every sealed bid once opened, who won and why, the foreman fee (never more than 5%), and their own share of the discount. Money for the month sits in a protected pot that the foreman cannot take.

The app speaks Telugu and English, uses rupees and grams only, works on low cost Android phones and slow networks, and every action ends with a simple receipt. Sign in is a phone number and a code.

## External FAQ (for customers)

**1. Is this real money?**
Not yet. Today every rupee and gram in Varanasi is test money in a demo. Nothing you do can lose or move real money. We will say clearly, in the app and by message, the day real money is allowed, and only after a bank partner and legal approvals are in place.

**2. Where is my gold kept?**
Planned: at a partner bank branch, tested in front of you, stored in the bank's vault, with a certificate number. Today: the "bank" in the demo is a test bank called "Mock Bank, Ameerpet branch (demo)". We do not have a bank partner yet.

**3. How do I know the gold is really there?**
The "Is my gold really there?" screen shows, for each bank, gold held against gold issued in the app, and turns red and stops new deposits if they ever do not match. Every certificate is listed.

**4. Can I get my gold back as real gold?**
Planned: yes, anyone holding grams can ask for gold at the branch. Jewellery deposited is melted and only pure gold counts, so we will say this clearly before anyone deposits a family piece.

**5. What is a chit and how is this one different?**
A chit is a group that pays a fixed amount each month; each month one member takes the pot. In Varanasi you see every payment, all sealed bids once opened, the winner and the reason, the foreman fee, and your share. The month's money is held in a protected pot, not in the foreman's pocket.

**6. What if I miss a payment?**
You see the late fee before you pay. If you keep missing, you can be removed and get back what you paid, minus the fee shown in your group rules. The app explains this in four short lines.

**7. Is this legal?**
Chits in India need State sanction and registration under the Chit Funds Act, 1982. Digital gold sold online is not regulated by SEBI today. We will not take real money until a registered foreman partner, a bank or SEBI registered vault manager partner, and a lawyer have approved the setup.

**8. Do I need to know about crypto or wallets?**
No. The app never asks you to buy crypto, hold a wallet, or pay network fees. We pay any system fees for you.

**9. What does it cost?**
Today: nothing; it is a demo. Real pricing will be shown in rupees before you agree to anything.

**10. What phone do I need?**
Any Android phone with a browser. The app is built to work on low cost phones and slow networks.

**11. What do you do with my phone number?**
On the waitlist we keep your number only to tell you about launch, and you can ask us to delete it. We never share it.

## Internal FAQ (for the team and investors)

**Who exactly is the customer?**
(1) Rural and semi urban households in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, often first time smartphone users, who already save in chits and small gold. (2) Salaried savers in Hyderabad (for example software engineers) who want gold and chit returns with full transparency.

**What is true today (Oct 11, 2026)?**
Contracts for bank attested gold, chit funds with sealed bid auctions, and gasless actions exist with a large passing test suite; a Next.js people app runs locally and as a demo on test money; Telugu and English toggle; waitlist built. Not true yet: any bank partner, any registered foreman, any real user, any regulatory approval, real SMS sign in, keys held only on the phone, native Telugu review, field test.

**What are the hardest problems?**
1. A bank or SEBI registered vault manager willing to sign gold certificates and handle take outs. 2. Chit regulation: State sanction per group, foreman security deposit, Registrar filings; banks may not run chits. 3. Rupee rails (UPI or bank escrow) for chit money. 4. Real sign in and key custody for people who will lose phones. 5. Trust and support in Telugu, including voice help.

**Why will customers switch?**
They will not switch for technology. They will switch if they can see their gold and their chit money clearly in Telugu, if it is cheaper or safer than the local alternative, and if someone they trust recommends it. The field test must prove this before we scale.

**What are we not building now?**
Lending against gold in production, a public token, anything that touches real money before the Bank pilot gate, any feature that needs English literacy to use.

**How will we measure success?**
Demo-ready: an investor can finish the gold and chit story on a phone in under 5 minutes in either language without help. Waitlist launch: signups by district, field test task success rate (target 8 of 10 people complete "pay this month" unaided), Telugu comprehension check. Bank pilot: signed partner, legal opinion, zero invariant breaks.

**What could make us stop?**
No bank or vault partner willing within the funding window; legal advice that the chit model cannot be operated by us or a partner; field test showing people do not trust an app with gold.
